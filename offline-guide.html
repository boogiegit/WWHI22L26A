Experiences

Venice - Gondolas & Grand Canal

EUR 96

Venice is beautiful from every angle, but from the water it becomes unforgettable. Glide along the Grand Canal by private water taxi, then slip into the quieter canals by gondola for the classic Venice moment nobody wants to miss.

Rome - Piazzas & Fountains of Rome + Pantheon Entrance

EUR 57

Rome is at its best when you wander its backstreets and suddenly land in a grand piazza or beside a beautiful fountain. Explore with a Local Guide and uncover the Eternal City's most charming corners, stories and photo moments.

Rome - Legends and Landmarks of Rome

EUR 46

Explore a quieter side of Rome with a Local Specialist, walking through the historic Jewish Quarter before discovering spectacular views over the Roman Forum — three thousand years of history in one photograph. Continue to the Capitoline Hill and Michelangelo’s beautifully designed Campidoglio square for a memorable insight into the stories, streets and landmarks of ancient Rome.

Rome - Inside the Colosseum & Roman Dinner

EUR 134

Step inside the mighty Colosseum, where gladiators once fought and Roman history still feels alive, then celebrate the final evening with a traditional local dinner. Ancient drama and hearty Roman flavour in one brilliant night.

Florence - Dinner in the Tuscan Countryside

EUR 80

Take a scenic drive to a friendly restaurant in the Tuscan countryside for a relaxed regional dinner with local ingredients and excellent wines from the surrounding vineyards.

Lucerne - Mount Pilatus & Lake Cruise

CHF 131

Ascend by cogwheel train to Mount Pilatus' summit, then descend by cable car and enjoy a scenic lake cruise surrounded by classic Swiss mountain views.

Lucerne - Swiss Countryside & Carriages

CHF 83

Take a step back in time with a horse-drawn carriage ride through rural Lucerne. Visit a local farm for fresh refreshments and experience the peace of Swiss country life.

Paris - La Nouvelle Eve Cabaret Show & Dinner

EUR 169

Make it a proper Paris night out at La Nouvelle Eve, with dinner, sparkle, feathers, music and the famous French can-can. It is glamorous, playful and exactly the sort of evening people talk about long after the tour ends.

Paris - Montmartre and Artists’ Village

EUR 50

Discover the village atmosphere of Montmartre, with its cobbled streets, welcoming cafés and rich artistic heritage. Explore Place du Tertre, where painters and portrait artists bring the square to life, and enjoy the charm of this much-loved Paris neighbourhood near Sacré-Cœur.

Paris - Seine Cruise & Paris Icons

EUR 51

Start Paris in style as the city lights up around you. Cruise the Seine past world-famous landmarks, then sweep through the streets by coach for those unforgettable first views of the Eiffel Tower, grand boulevards and glittering Paris icons.

Joining & FAQ

Before Departure

Should I take any health precautions?

Check any personal health or medication questions with your healthcare professional before travel. Keep essential medication and your travel insurance details in your carry-on.

How strict are the suitcase size guidelines?

They're guidelines - don't stress about an inch here or there. That said, the coach doesn't have elastic sides, so please pack reasonably. Travelling lighter makes life easier, especially with early morning bag collections.

What if my luggage doesn't arrive at the airport?

Fill out a report at the baggage claim desk immediately and include all your hotels on it. Always photograph your suitcase before check-in.

Arrival in London

Where does this trip start?

This Xmas Whirl trip starts in London. Day 1 is Tuesday 22 December 2026, travelling by Eurostar from London St Pancras to Brussels, then by coach to Amsterdam. Please follow the London joining instructions in your booking documents.

Where do I meet an airport transfer?

Check your booking documents for your London arrival airport, whether a transfer is included, and its meeting point and time. London has several airports, so there is no single terminal or meeting point for everyone. If anything is unclear, contact your booking agent before travelling.

Which London hotel should I go to?

Use the pre-tour London hotel and address shown in your joining documents. The Day 1 hotel in this app is in Amsterdam, where we stay after travelling from London. Your pre-tour London hotel has not yet been confirmed in the app.

What if I arrive late or miss my transfer?

Use the assistance number in your travel documents and tell your booking agent or Travel Director. If you need to arrange your own transport, confirm the London hotel address first and use official airport transport or a licensed taxi. Allow enough time to join the early Day 1 departure.

What do I need for the Eurostar journey?

Keep your passport and travel documents in your day bag. Follow Brendon’s latest meeting and departure instructions in Today. Allow time for station check-in and border checks; your joining documents and live updates take priority over the saved itinerary.

Money & Payments

Which currencies will I need?

Use pounds sterling in London, euros in the euro-area countries on our route, and Swiss francs in Switzerland. Bring a suitable payment card and some local cash. Check overseas fees with your bank before departure.

Do I need to book optional experiences in advance?

No - you'll book these with Brendon once the trip is underway. Come with an open mind and an appetite for the extraordinary.

Hotels

What can I expect from European hotels?

Rooms are smaller than North American equivalents. Beds are usually standard doubles, not king size. Insert your key card into the slot inside your room to activate electricity - keep it away from your phone. No irons in most hotels.

Is porterage included?

Yes - one bag per person, included. Your suitcase is collected each morning and delivered to your next room. Keep a day bag with everything you need during the day.

What about breakfast?

Breakfast and luggage times vary by day and hotel. Check Today and Brendon’s latest announcements; Day 1 has an early departure from London.

Packing

What should I pack in my carry-on?

Valuables, a change of clothes, prescription medication, and anything you cannot be separated from. Airlines do lose luggage - especially on connecting flights.

What is the dress code?

Almost everything is come-as-you-are, but pack one smart outfit for special dinners. Churches require covered shoulders and knees - a scarf is your best friend. Shoes must be comfortable and already broken-in before you arrive.

What about electrical adapters?

Bring adapters suitable for the UK and continental Europe, including Switzerland. Check that your chargers support the local voltage. Keep your phone charger and power bank in your day bag.

What does the hotel NOT provide?

Washcloths, hair conditioner, and kettles (becoming more common but not guaranteed). Hairdryers provided but usually basic.

Connectivity

How do I manage my phone abroad?

Make sure your phone is set up for international roaming before you leave. An eSIM often offers better value - Airalo is a reliable provider with European data plans. Keep photos of your passport and documents on your phone and also email them to yourself.

Will there be Wi-Fi?

Hotel Wi-Fi quality varies. Check that your mobile plan covers the UK, the European countries on the route and Switzerland. Save the offline guide while connected so you can read the itinerary and hotel details without mobile data.
